**Muse Wonder** — and can I say what a wonder of a model. It writes very, very well. It's no **Opus 5.5**, but it's a genuine top-3 writer right now (writing is subjective though — might hate it).

Second pre-train class out of **Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL)** after Muse Spark. Codename **Watermelon** (Spark was Avocado — "we're out of fruits," per Zuckerberg). Two variants: **Wonder** (API) and **Wonder Full** (subscription-only — not the same as max effort on Wonder).

Frontier personal + professional agent model: memory, task persistence, domain expertise (legal/finance/health), long-horizon coding.

**Issues:** The same problems that steer me away from Meta models beyond **Muse Glimmer 30B** — they do not stream their reasoning. It's all hidden backend. The entire point of a reasoning model is seeing how it thinks through problems; it's hard to steer various tasks when you don't know where the model is getting hung up. Pathetic really.
